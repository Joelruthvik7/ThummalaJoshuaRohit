THE BROTHER'S LETTER

Happy Birthday, Peddhannaya

Wishing you many more happy returns of the day, Peddhannaya. May God bless you with endless happiness, good health, peace, wisdom, success, and abundant blessings throughout your life. I pray that every dream in your heart and every prayer you have carried silently before God will be fulfilled in His perfect timing.

You are one of the strongest people I know and the true backbone of our family. Many people may never fully understand the sacrifices you have made, the responsibilities you have carried, or the battles you have fought in silence. Some may speak without knowing your story, but I truly believe that one day they will recognize your value, your heart, and your character. The same people who misunderstand you today will one day respect the man you have become.

From your childhood, Mom always prayed for you and dedicated you to God. I strongly believe that God's hand has always been upon your life. Every struggle, every delay, every disappointment, and every challenge has been part of His plan to shape you into the person you are today.

THE CORE ESSENCE

"You genuinely love people. You trust people. You support people."

Whenever someone is struggling, you are always willing to help. Many people never get to see the kindness that exists in your heart. The sacrifices you make, the support you give, and the care you show often go unnoticed.

I am different in some ways. I tend to observe people, understand their intentions, and decide how close I should be based on their actions and character. But you are not like that. You choose to see the good in people. You believe in them. You encourage them. You give them chances.

That is your greatness. That is your strength. And sometimes, that same quality can become your weakness. But in today's world, it is rare to find someone with a genuinely pure heart, sincere love, and a selfless desire to help others. You are one of those rare people.

Happy Birthday, Peddhannaya!

Joel Ruthvik
